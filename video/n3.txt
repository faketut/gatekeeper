Here is the live demo. A forty-nine dollar daytime purchase scores five point seven percent: allow. A two-thousand dollar order at three a.m. from a new device scores forty-five percent: review — it waits in the approval queue, and nothing moves until a human says yes. A twelve-thousand dollar three a.m. burst scores seventy-eight point eight percent: blocked outright. These demo thresholds, thirty-five and seventy-five percent, are the behavioural scorer's — not the trained model's.
